No countries appear

ATLAS needs photos with a location. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → ATLAS. You can also add journeys by hand from the Journeys tab, or explore with Demo Mode (You → Settings & privacy).

A place or a journey is wrong

Open it and tap Edit or Correct this place. Your corrections are kept when ATLAS reads your library again.

Widgets show old information

Widgets show ATLAS as it was the last time you opened the app. Open ATLAS to refresh them.

Deleting your data

You → Settings & privacy → Delete all ATLAS data. Your photos are never touched. See the privacy policy.
